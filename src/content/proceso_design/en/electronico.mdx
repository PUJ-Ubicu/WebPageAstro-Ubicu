Analysis, modeling, simulation and construction of the incentive respiratory system were carried out to ensure the product's functional requirements are met, and its operation was validated across the entire instrumentation chain. The instrumentation chain includes: the primary sensor of the measured variable, the measurement-signal transducer, the conditioning module, and digitization. Related aspects such as signal processing and transmission are also considered. The design process was carried out in a modular way while meeting the product's functional requirements, which ensures reliable operation and efficient fault analysis.
