Serious games are used to support physiotherapy processes and give the patient an environment to perform the exercises in an engaging way that reduces the perception of pain. There is no evidence of videogames dedicated to recovering or preventing the loss of lung capacity from various diseases while at the same time giving the therapist the ability to manage several patients remotely. Two software tools were developed: one for interacting with the patient through the game, and another for the remote administration and prescription of patients by the therapist. After testing with users (not patients), a potential for greater adherence was found with the use of the game, suggesting its potential for application and recovery in patients.
